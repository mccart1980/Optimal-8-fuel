# FUEL — THE SEASON · PHASES AND SWAPS

**The fuel plan was written for one week repeated. The training is now a season — build, heavy, fast, test week, camp, fight week, transition — and the food has to follow it, because the right amount of food for a volume block is the wrong amount for a taper. This document does two things: it makes the plan phase-aware, and it gives every feed of the day a set of swaps that match it, so the timing and the numbers hold whatever you eat.**

## THE PRINCIPLE, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Fuel for the work required. That's the whole of modern sports nutrition in four words, and the research behind it is consistent: match carbohydrate to the training load of the day and the block, keep protein high and steady across the year, hold fat at its floor, and move energy up in blocks where tissue is being built and down where it isn't. Building blocks get a bigger surplus because muscle is being made; heavy and fast blocks get a small surplus because strength and speed don't need mass; test weeks and tapers keep the carbohydrate up while the training drops, because glycogen refills when the work stops and that's the tank you walk into the test or the ring with; transitions eat at maintenance to hold the muscle and let the body reset; and a weight cut, if there is one, is done early, slowly, and never from protein. The meal pattern — six feeds, the bottle around the session, the 5pm loads, casein before bed — stays the same in every phase, because it's built on the shape of your training days and those don't change. The amounts change. That's what the phase table does.

## THE PHASE TABLE — WHAT CHANGES, AND WHEN

The base day is Menu A as written: ~3,600 kcal average, 245 g protein, 487 g carbs, 78 g fat. Every phase is a change to that, and nothing else moves.

| Phase | When | Daily average | The change from Menu A |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BUILD** (Prep accumulation) | Prep weeks 1–5, or 1–6 in the 16-week form | ~3,900 · P245 · C560 · F80 | Add a CARB TOP-UP at 17:00 on Monday and Thursday — the two days without one. Surplus about 300. Tissue is being built; feed it. The tape at the block's end decides whether it stays. |
| **HEAVY** (intensify) | Prep weeks 6–10 | ~3,600 | Menu A as written. The extra top-ups come off. |
| **FAST** (convert) | Prep weeks 11–14 | ~3,600 | Menu A as written. On the scored round weeks, the mid-session banana. |
| **TEST WEEK** | Prep's last week | ~3,600 | Do not cut. Training drops, carbs hold; the tank fills. Test day eats like a Saturday. |
| **CAMP** (foundation, build, peak) | The ten weeks | ~3,600 | Menu A. Seven-round weeks keep the mid-session banana. Sauna weeks: the hydration schedule's sauna line. |
| **CAMP — easy week and sharpen** | | ~3,600 | Do not cut. The commonest way to lose a fight is eating less because you're training less. |
| **FIGHT WEEK** | | ~3,600 | Menu A exactly. Weigh-in day per the weight section. |
| **TRANSITION** | The two weeks after a fight | ~3,300 · P235 · C420 · F78 | Drop the 3pm feed on Monday, Wednesday and Thursday and the Saturday BIG portion back to standard. Maintenance. Protein holds so the muscle does. |
| **MAKING WEIGHT** | Only when the limit demands it, decided in camp week 1 | ~3,100 · P200 · C400 · F70 | The Fuel plan's making-weight section: carbs off the light days, never protein, never the bottle, never the 5pm loads. Half a percent of bodyweight a week, no faster. |

**The medication window.** While appetite is suppressed, nothing in the table changes; the liquid feeds go down when the solid ones won't, the schedule replaces hunger, and the Sunday scale is the alarm — more than half a kilo down in a week and the top-up goes back on regardless of phase.

Sit-down options need a microwave or a plate; on-the-move options need a bag. Anything new here is on the list because it earns its place; nothing is on it for variety's sake.

## HOW TO USE IT

Pick by slot, not by day. Breakfast is A on a normal morning and C on the one you can't face porridge; lunch is A when you're sat down and B or C when you're not; dinner rotates through A, B, C and D across the week with the steak nights on the heavy days. The app holds every option under its slot with a tap, the phase sets the number of top-ups and the size of the weekend feeds, and the totals recompute whatever you choose. The three rules stay: the bottle around every session, the 5pm loads never move, the tape decides.

## THE SHOP — WHAT CHANGES

Menu A's list stays. For the swaps, add as you use them: bagels and wraps as Menu B says; tuna, four tins a week; potatoes if you take the jacket or the potato top-up; salmon, two 200 g fillets a week if you'll eat it; lean turkey mince, 500 g if you take the chilli; dates, a small bag; oats and butter for the flapjack. Buy the swaps you'll actually take, not all of them.
