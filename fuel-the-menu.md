# FUEL — OPTIMAL 8 · THE MENU

**Every feed of the day, three to six ways, all matched to the number the feed has to hit. Pick by the slot, not by the day. The times don't move, the totals don't move; only what's on the plate does.**

## THE FIVE RULES

1. **Half the bottle and a carb twenty minutes before every session; the other half straight after.** Every day you train.
2. **The 5pm load on Tuesday, Friday and Saturday never moves.** It's loading tomorrow's session. Low fat, low fibre, easy to move on.
3. **Every feed, every day, whether you're hungry or not.** The plan is the plan; the tape is the referee.
4. **The phase sets the amounts.** The app knows the block you're in and adds or removes a feed. Don't add anything back.
5. **Drink when the alarm says.** The litre with a sachet at work start, empty by nine.

Friday has no session: breakfast on waking, then the day as printed. Saturday and Sunday: breakfast big at 06:30, the bottle at 07:45, the other half with two bananas straight after, then the feeds through the day.

## WHY THESE FOODS

Carbohydrate is matched to the work: dense and fast around the sessions (bananas, dried fruit, rice cakes, honey, white rice, bagels), slower and with fibre away from them (oats, wholemeal wraps, jacket potatoes, beans, apples). Protein is 30–45 g at every feed because that's the dose that switches muscle-building on and the gap of three hours is the spacing that keeps it on. Fat sits at its floor from eggs and steak and rises nowhere else, because fat around a session slows the fuel down. Fish is in twice a week if you'll eat it — salmon for the omega-3 the capsules only imitate; cod, prawns and scallops for the most protein per calorie of anything on the list, plus iodine and selenium the rest of the diet lacks. Dried fruit is fuel, not a snack: brilliant before and after training and in the 5pm load, wrong anywhere else in quantity. Beans go with lunch, never with the load. Tuna stays at four tins a week. Nothing is on this list for variety's sake; every option earns its slot by its numbers.

## THE BATCH, THE PREP, THE SHOP

The mince pot and the sweet potato cooked separately, weighed once, cooked, in the COOK tab; four mince-only tubs for the wraps. Twelve eggs boiled Sunday, nine Wednesday — a week in the shell. Two bagels, a tin and a pack of rice cakes live in the work bag. Prawns and cod: buy frozen, cook from frozen in ten minutes; scallops fresh, seared two minutes a side. Wraps rolled the night before, in a cool bag with a freezer block.

Weekly on top of the standing list, as you use them: bagels 10 · wholemeal wraps 10 · rice cakes 2 packs · raisins or sultanas 500 g · apples 7 · tuna 4 tins · beans 2 tins · potatoes 2 kg · frozen prawns 1 kg · cod or salmon 2 × 250 g · scallops when you want them · dates a small bag.

## HYDRATION — THE SHORT VERSION

500 ml on waking · 500 in the session · 300 with breakfast, the creatine in it · **the litre with a sachet at work start, empty by nine** · 500 with each feed, 300 in the gaps · urine check at ten and four, pale straw or drink now · sachet two at three only on hot days or after a dark check · 250 at eight, nothing big after. Weekends: 750 with a sachet through the session, 500 before and after the sauna. About four and a half litres on a work day.
